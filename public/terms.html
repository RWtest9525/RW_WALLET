Terms and Safety

RW Wallet is intended for Reviews World registered users. Users should keep their login details private and only use the official app URL.

Important Safety Rules

RW Wallet will never ask for OTPs, card PINs, seed phrases, bank passwords, or remote access to your device. If anyone asks for these details, do not share them.

Wallet Requests

Wallet, loan, recharge, withdrawal, and partner requests are reviewed and processed according to admin rules and account status.

Admin Review

Some actions require admin approval. Approval, limits, wallet balance updates, and account restrictions are shown inside the app for the related registered user.

Contact

For account or safety help, contact reviewsworld01@gmail.com.

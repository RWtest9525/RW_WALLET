Privacy Policy

RW Wallet is a Reviews World app for registered users. We collect account details only to manage wallet activity, requests, support, and verification inside this app.

Information We Use

Name, email, mobile number, wallet activity, request history, support messages, and verification documents when a user submits them for admin review.

How It Is Used

Information is used for account access, admin verification, wallet records, user support, and fraud prevention. We do not ask for bank passwords, card PINs, OTPs, seed phrases, or browser passwords.

Data Safety

Users should open only the official RW Wallet URL and should never share OTPs, payment PINs, card details, or remote access with anyone claiming to be support.

Contact

For privacy or safety concerns, contact reviewsworld01@gmail.com.
